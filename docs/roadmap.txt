TechNova Solutions – Product Roadmap (2025-2027)

Q3 2025 (Completed):
- NovaChat AI 3.0: Added multi-language support (12 languages), sentiment analysis, and conversation summarization.
- NovaCloud: Launched auto-scaling for GPU workloads and added South America region (São Paulo).
- NovaCRM: Released email campaign builder and lead scoring module.

Q4 2025 (Completed):
- NovaAnalytics 2.5: Added predictive forecasting, anomaly detection, and custom dashboard templates.
- NovaAutomation: Launched visual workflow builder with drag-and-drop interface.
- NovaSecure 1.0: Initial release with vulnerability scanning, DDoS protection, and compliance monitoring.

Q1 2026 (Current):
- NovaAssist 2.0: Internal helpdesk AI with IT ticket automation, HR self-service, and knowledge base search.
- NovaChat AI: Added voice channel support and WhatsApp integration.
- Platform: Unified single sign-on (SSO) across all Nova products.

Q2 2026 (Planned):
- NovaCRM 3.0: AI-powered sales forecasting, pipeline management, and customer health scores.
- NovaCloud: Edge computing support and Kubernetes managed service.
- NovaAnalytics: Natural language querying – ask questions in plain English and get charts.

Q3 2026 (Planned):
- NovaAutomation 3.0: AI-assisted workflow creation – describe what you want to automate in natural language.
- NovaSecure 2.0: Zero-trust architecture, insider threat detection, and SOC-as-a-service.
- Mobile Apps: iOS and Android apps for NovaCRM, NovaChat AI, and NovaAssist.

Q4 2026 (Planned):
- Nova Platform Marketplace: Third-party integrations and plugins marketplace.
- NovaChat AI 4.0: Multi-modal AI (text, voice, image, and video).
- Enterprise Data Lake: Unified data platform connecting all Nova products.

2027 Vision:
- Full AI-native platform where every product learns and adapts from usage patterns.
- Autonomous operations: Self-healing infrastructure, self-optimizing workflows, and proactive customer support.
- Industry-specific solutions for financial services, professional services, and telecommunications.
