TechNova Solutions – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

TechNova Solutions is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect:
- Account information: Name, email, company name, phone number, billing address.
- Usage data: Product usage patterns, feature interactions, login history.
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, cookies.
- Communication data: Support tickets, emails, chat conversations.
- Payment data: Credit card information (processed by Stripe, never stored on our servers).

How We Use Your Data:
- To provide and improve our products and services.
- To communicate with you about your account, updates, and support.
- To personalize your experience and recommend relevant features.
- To ensure security and prevent fraud.
- To comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing:
We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- Service providers (AWS, Stripe, Datadog) necessary to deliver our services.
- Law enforcement when required by law.
- Business partners only with your explicit consent.

Data Retention:
We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, we retain data for 90 days for recovery purposes, then permanently delete it.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA):
- Right to access: Request a copy of your data.
- Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate data.
- Right to deletion: Request deletion of your data.
- Right to portability: Export your data in a standard format.
- Right to opt-out: Opt out of marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@technovasolutions.com.

Cookies:
We use essential cookies for authentication and security, and optional analytics cookies to improve our products. You can manage cookie preferences from your browser settings.
