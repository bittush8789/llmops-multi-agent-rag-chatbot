TechNova Solutions - HR Common Queries

Q: What is the policy for 'Work from Anywhere'?
A: Employees can work from any location for up to 30 days per year, provided it doesn't conflict with tax residency laws and has manager approval.

Q: How do I submit an expense report?
A: Use the 'Expenses' module in NovaAssist. Attach receipts for any item over $25. Approval typically takes 3-5 business days.

Q: Does TechNova offer tuition reimbursement?
A: Yes, we offer up to $5,000 per year for degree-related programs that align with your career path at TechNova.

Q: How do I change my 401(k) contribution?
A: Log in to the 'Fidelity NetBenefits' portal (accessible via SSO from NovaAssist). Changes take effect in the next pay cycle.
