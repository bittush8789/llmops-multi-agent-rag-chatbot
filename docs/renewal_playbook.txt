TechNova Solutions - Customer Renewal Playbook

Objective: Ensure 100% net revenue retention.

Phase 1: Performance Review (90 days out)
- Audit usage statistics (NovaAnalytics).
- Review all open and resolved support tickets.
- Prepare ROI report based on saved hours and reduced costs.

Phase 2: Strategy Meeting (60 days out)
- Present ROI report to key stakeholders.
- Identify pain points or new requirements.
- Introduce new features from the TechNova roadmap (e.g., NovaSecure 2.0).

Phase 3: Proposal & Negotiation (45 days out)
- Provide renewal proposal with multi-year discount options.
- Address any pricing or feature concerns.

Phase 4: Contract Signature (30 days out)
- Finalize legal and procurement details.
- Transition account to next-year success plan.
