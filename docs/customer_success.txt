TechNova Solutions - Customer Success Framework

Customer Onboarding Process:
- Week 1: Kick-off meeting, goals definition, and technical audit.
- Week 2: Knowledge base ingestion and initial training of NovaChat AI.
- Week 3: User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and feedback loops.
- Week 4: Production rollout and team training.

Success Metrics (KPIs):
- Time-to-Value: Average 28 days for full platform rollout.
- CSAT: Target score of 4.8/5 for support interactions.
- Usage Adoption: 80% active users within first 90 days.
- Cost Savings: Average 30% reduction in customer support tickets after NovaChat AI deployment.

Renewal Playbook:
- 90 Days Before Renewal: Quarterly Business Review (QBR) to show ROI metrics.
- 60 Days Before Renewal: Identification of expansion opportunities (additional products or users).
- 30 Days Before Renewal: Contract finalization and signature.

Common Complaints & Resolutions:
- "Slow response time": Usually due to large PDF ingestion; resolve by optimizing chunking or switching to high-compute NovaCloud instances.
- "Inaccurate answers": Resolve by cleaning up the knowledge base or adjusting the retriever's top_k parameters.
