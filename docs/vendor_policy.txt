TechNova Solutions – Vendor Policy

Vendor Selection Criteria:
TechNova selects vendors based on quality, reliability, security posture, pricing competitiveness, and alignment with our values. All vendors must pass a security and compliance review before engagement.

Vendor Onboarding:
1. Submit vendor application at vendors.technovasolutions.com
2. Complete security questionnaire and NDA
3. Provide references from 3 existing enterprise clients
4. Undergo compliance review (SOC 2 or equivalent required)
5. Contract negotiation and MSA execution
6. Onboarding and integration setup

Payment Terms:
- Standard payment terms are Net 30 from invoice date
- Large contracts may negotiate Net 45 or Net 60
- All payments processed via wire transfer or ACH
- Invoices must be submitted to ap@technovasolutions.com

Vendor Code of Conduct:
All vendors must adhere to our Vendor Code of Conduct which includes ethical business practices, anti-corruption compliance, data protection obligations, environmental responsibility, and fair labor practices.

Vendor Performance Reviews:
We conduct quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with all strategic vendors to assess performance, address issues, and align on future plans. Vendors are rated on delivery quality, responsiveness, innovation, and value.

Preferred Vendors:
- Cloud Infrastructure: AWS, Google Cloud, Azure
- Security Tools: CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks
- Communication: Slack, Zoom
- HR/Payroll: Workday, Gusto
- DevOps: GitHub, CircleCI, Datadog
