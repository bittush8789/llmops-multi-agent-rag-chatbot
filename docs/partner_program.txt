TechNova Solutions – Partner Program

NovaPartner Program Overview:
The NovaPartner Program enables technology partners, consultants, and resellers to grow their business by offering TechNova solutions to their clients. Partners earn commissions, receive training, and get access to co-marketing resources.

Partner Tiers:

Silver Partner:
- Requirements: 3+ certified consultants, 5+ customer referrals per year
- Benefits: 10% revenue share, partner portal access, basic training, co-branded materials
- Support: Email-based partner support with 24-hour response time

Gold Partner:
- Requirements: 5+ certified consultants, 15+ customer referrals per year, $250K+ influenced revenue
- Benefits: 15% revenue share, dedicated partner manager, priority support, joint webinars, lead sharing
- Support: Dedicated Slack channel, 4-hour response time

Platinum Partner:
- Requirements: 10+ certified consultants, 30+ customer referrals per year, $1M+ influenced revenue
- Benefits: 20% revenue share, executive sponsorship, custom integrations support, annual partner summit invitation, early access to new products
- Support: Named partner success manager, 1-hour response time

Technology Partners:
We integrate with leading platforms including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira, ServiceNow, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Snowflake, and Databricks. Technology partners receive API access, sandbox environments, and engineering support.

Consulting Partners:
System integrators and consulting firms can become certified to implement TechNova products. We provide implementation playbooks, training academies, and project management support.

How to Apply:
Visit partners.technovasolutions.com or email partnerships@technovasolutions.com. Applications are reviewed within 5 business days.
