TechNova Solutions – Service Level Agreement (SLA)

TechNova provides SLA guarantees for all paid plans. SLA commitments vary by plan tier.

Uptime Guarantees:
- Starter Plan: 99.5% monthly uptime
- Pro Plan: 99.9% monthly uptime
- Business Plan: 99.95% monthly uptime
- Enterprise Plan: 99.99% monthly uptime (custom SLA available)

Uptime is measured monthly. Scheduled maintenance windows (announced 48 hours in advance) are excluded from uptime calculations.

Service Credits:
If we fail to meet our uptime commitment, you are eligible for service credits:
- 99.0% - 99.5%: 10% credit of monthly fee
- 98.0% - 99.0%: 25% credit of monthly fee
- Below 98.0%: 50% credit of monthly fee

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident by emailing sla@technovasolutions.com.

Support Response Times:
- Starter: 24-hour email response (business days)
- Pro: 4-hour response (24/7)
- Business: 2-hour response (24/7)
- Enterprise: 1-hour response (24/7), 15-minute for P1

Resolution Targets:
- P1 (Critical outage): 4-hour resolution target
- P2 (Major issue): 8-hour resolution target
- P3 (Minor issue): 24-hour resolution target
- P4 (General inquiry): 48-hour resolution target

Data Backup:
- Automated daily backups for all plans
- 30-day backup retention (Standard), 90-day (Enterprise)
- Point-in-time recovery available for Enterprise plans
- Cross-region backup replication for Enterprise plans

Disaster Recovery:
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours (Standard), 1 hour (Enterprise)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 24 hours (Standard), 1 hour (Enterprise)

Contact: sla@technovasolutions.com
