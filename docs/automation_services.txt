TechNova Solutions – Automation Services

We help organizations automate repetitive, manual processes using NovaAutomation and custom AI-powered workflows.

What We Automate:
- Data entry and data migration between systems
- Invoice processing and accounts payable
- Employee onboarding and offboarding workflows
- Report generation and distribution
- Customer communication and follow-ups
- IT ticket routing and resolution
- Compliance monitoring and alerting
- Contract review and approval workflows

Our Approach:

1. Process Discovery: We observe and document your current manual processes, identify bottlenecks, and calculate the time and cost of each process.

2. Automation Design: We design the automated workflow, define triggers, actions, conditions, and error handling. We use NovaAutomation's visual workflow builder for transparency.

3. Build and Test: We build the automation, connect to your systems via APIs or RPA bots, and test thoroughly in a staging environment.

4. Deploy and Monitor: We deploy to production with rollback capabilities. We monitor automation performance and handle exceptions.

5. Optimize: We continuously improve automations based on performance data, edge cases, and changing business requirements.

ROI Examples:
- Invoice Processing: Reduced processing time from 15 minutes to 30 seconds per invoice. Saved 2,000 hours/year.
- Employee Onboarding: Automated 23 manual steps. Reduced onboarding time from 5 days to 4 hours.
- Report Generation: Automated daily sales reports across 12 regions. Eliminated 3 hours of daily manual work.
