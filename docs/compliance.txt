TechNova Solutions – Compliance

TechNova maintains compliance with major industry standards and regulations to ensure our customers can trust us with their most sensitive data.

SOC 2 Type II:
TechNova is SOC 2 Type II certified. Our annual audit covers the Trust Service Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy. Audit reports are available to customers under NDA upon request.

ISO 27001:
Our Information Security Management System (ISMS) is ISO 27001 certified. We undergo annual surveillance audits and full recertification every 3 years.

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):
TechNova is fully GDPR compliant. We act as a Data Processor for customer data and provide Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) to all EU customers. We support data subject rights including access, rectification, deletion, and portability.

HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act):
For healthcare clients, TechNova offers HIPAA-compliant deployments with Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). This includes dedicated infrastructure, enhanced audit logging, and additional access controls.

CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act):
We comply with CCPA requirements including the right to know, right to delete, and right to opt-out. Our privacy policy details our data practices transparently.

PCI DSS:
Payment card data is processed through PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor (Stripe). TechNova never stores raw credit card numbers on our systems.

Data Residency:
Enterprise customers can choose their data residency region: US, EU, or APAC. All data remains within the selected region and is not transferred across borders without explicit consent.

Compliance Documents Available:
- SOC 2 Type II Report
- ISO 27001 Certificate
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
- Penetration Test Summary
- Privacy Impact Assessment
