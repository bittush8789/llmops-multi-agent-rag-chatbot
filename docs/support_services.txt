TechNova Solutions – Support Services

We offer multi-tier technical support to ensure our customers get the help they need, when they need it.

Support Channels:
- Email: support@technovasolutions.com
- Phone: 1-800-555-NOVA (6682)
- Live Chat: Available on all product dashboards
- Support Portal: support.technovasolutions.com (ticket tracking, knowledge base, community forum)

Support Tiers:

Standard Support (included with Starter plan):
- Email support only
- Response time: 24 hours (business days)
- Knowledge base access
- Community forum access

Priority Support (included with Pro plan):
- Email, phone, and live chat
- Response time: 4 hours (24/7)
- Dedicated support queue
- Screen sharing and remote troubleshooting

Enterprise Support (included with Enterprise plan):
- All Priority features
- Response time: 1 hour (24/7)
- Named Support Engineer
- Quarterly support reviews
- Custom escalation procedures
- On-site support available

Premium Support Add-on ($500/month):
- 15-minute response time for critical issues (P1)
- Technical Account Manager
- Proactive monitoring and alerting
- Monthly health checks

Issue Severity Levels:
- P1 (Critical): Complete service outage affecting all users. Target resolution: 4 hours.
- P2 (High): Major feature unavailable, workaround exists. Target resolution: 8 hours.
- P3 (Medium): Minor issue, limited impact. Target resolution: 24 hours.
- P4 (Low): General question or feature request. Target resolution: 48 hours.

Escalation Process:
If you are not satisfied with the support you receive, you can escalate by emailing escalations@technovasolutions.com or asking your Customer Success Manager to intervene.
