TechNova Solutions – Hiring Process

We hire the best talent globally. Our process is designed to be fair, efficient, and transparent.

Open Positions:
All open roles are listed on careers.technovasolutions.com. We hire for Engineering, Product, Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, HR, Finance, Legal, and Operations.

Application Process:
1. Apply Online: Submit your resume and cover letter through our careers portal.
2. Recruiter Screen (30 min): A talent acquisition specialist reviews your background, experience, and salary expectations.
3. Hiring Manager Interview (45 min): A deep dive into your experience, skills, and culture fit with the hiring manager.
4. Technical/Skills Assessment (60 min): For engineering roles, this includes a live coding exercise. For other roles, a case study or work sample.
5. Team Panel Interview (60 min): Meet 2-3 future teammates for a collaborative discussion.
6. Final Interview with VP/Director (30 min): A conversation about career goals, growth, and alignment with TechNova's mission.
7. Offer: Offers are typically extended within 48 hours of the final interview.

Timeline: The full process takes 2-3 weeks on average.

Referral Program: Employees earn a $5,000 referral bonus for successful hires. The bonus is paid after the new hire completes 90 days.

Internship Program: We run a 12-week summer internship program for university students. Interns work on real projects with mentorship from senior engineers and product managers. Over 60% of interns receive full-time return offers.

Relocation Support: For candidates relocating to a new city, TechNova provides a $10,000 relocation stipend, temporary housing assistance for 30 days, and visa sponsorship where applicable.

Onboarding: New hires go through a structured 2-week onboarding program called "NovaLaunch" that covers company culture, product training, tools setup, and team introductions.
