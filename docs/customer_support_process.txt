TechNova Solutions - Customer Support Process

1. Ticket Submission:
- Customers can submit tickets via email, phone, or the NovaAssist portal.
- All tickets are automatically categorized by the NovaAssist AI.

2. Triage:
- Level 1 Support: Handles general inquiries and known issues (Billing, Basic Troubleshooting).
- Level 2 Support: Handles complex technical issues (API integration, Cloud performance).
- Level 3 Support: Senior Engineering team for core system bugs.

3. SLA Management:
- Tickets are prioritized based on the customer's plan (Enterprise > Pro > Starter).
- P1 issues are escalated to management after 2 hours without resolution.

4. Feedback Loop:
- After ticket closure, a CSAT survey is sent to the user.
- Feedback is reviewed weekly by the Customer Success team.

5. Escalation:
- If a customer is unsatisfied, they can request a 'Manager Callback' or contact their dedicated Account Manager (Enterprise only).
