TechNova Solutions – Product Onboarding Guide

Getting Started with TechNova Products:

Step 1: Create Your Account
Visit app.technovasolutions.com and click "Start Free Trial." Enter your business email, company name, and select your plan. No credit card required for the 14-day trial.

Step 2: Choose Your Products
During signup, select which products you'd like to activate. You can add or remove products later from the Admin Dashboard.

Step 3: Connect Your Data
- NovaChat AI: Upload your knowledge base documents (PDFs, docs, web URLs). The AI will process and index them within 30 minutes.
- NovaCloud: Connect your existing cloud accounts or provision new infrastructure directly.
- NovaAnalytics: Add data sources by entering connection details. We support 50+ connectors.
- NovaCRM: Import contacts via CSV or connect to Salesforce/HubSpot for automatic sync.
- NovaAutomation: Browse pre-built workflow templates or create your first automation.
- NovaAssist: Connect to Jira/ServiceNow for IT tickets and Workday/BambooHR for HR data.
- NovaSecure: Install the security agent on your infrastructure for vulnerability scanning.

Step 4: Invite Your Team
Add team members from the Admin Dashboard. Assign roles (Admin, Manager, User, Viewer) and configure SSO if available.

Step 5: Customize
Apply your company branding (logo, colors, domain) to customer-facing products like NovaChat AI and NovaCRM.

Step 6: Go Live
Once testing is complete, switch from sandbox to production mode. Our onboarding team is available for go-live support.

Onboarding Support:
All new customers receive a dedicated onboarding specialist for the first 30 days. Schedule your kickoff call at onboarding.technovasolutions.com.
