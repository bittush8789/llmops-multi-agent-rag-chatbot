TechNova Solutions – Travel Policy

Business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager. All travel should be booked through our corporate travel portal at travel.technovasolutions.com.

Air Travel:
- Domestic flights: Economy class for flights under 5 hours. Premium economy for flights over 5 hours.
- International flights: Premium economy for flights under 8 hours. Business class for flights over 8 hours.
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible to get the best rates.

Hotels:
- Maximum nightly rate: $250 (US domestic), $300 (international).
- Book through the corporate portal for negotiated rates.
- Extended stays (5+ nights): Contact the travel team for serviced apartment options.

Ground Transportation:
- Use ride-sharing services (Uber, Lyft) for local transportation.
- Rental cars are approved for trips where public transportation is impractical.
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use: $0.67/mile.

Meals:
- Per diem: $75/day (US domestic), $100/day (international).
- Client entertainment meals: Up to $150/person with manager pre-approval.
- Alcohol is limited to 2 drinks per meal during client entertainment.

Expense Reporting:
- Submit expense reports within 14 days of trip completion via NovaAssist.
- Attach receipts for all expenses over $25.
- Reimbursements are processed within 5 business days of approval.

Travel Safety:
- Register all international trips with the security team.
- Do not travel to countries on the company restricted list without VP and security approval.
- Company travel insurance covers medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and baggage loss.

Contact: travel@technovasolutions.com
