TechNova Solutions - Sales Playbook

Enterprise Sales Strategy:
1. Discovery: Identify pain points in current AI or cloud infrastructure. Common issues: high costs, low accuracy, scaling bottlenecks.
2. Value Proposition: Highlight TechNova's integrated ecosystem. NovaChat AI + NovaCloud = Seamless scalability.
3. Objections Handling:
   - "It's too expensive": Show ROI examples (40% cost reduction at Acme Corp).
   - "Security concerns": Mention SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance.
   - "Implementation time": Show 4-week rapid deployment timeline for NovaChat AI.

Upsell Opportunities:
- Starter to Pro: Mention NovaAnalytics and 24/7 priority support.
- Pro to Enterprise: Focus on dedicated account management, custom SLAs, and multi-region deployment.
- Cross-sell: If they use NovaChat AI, suggest NovaAutomation for back-office tasks.

Competitive Advantage:
- Unlike generic LLMs, NovaChat AI is grounded in YOUR private data.
- 99.99% uptime guarantee.
- Human-in-the-loop validation prevents hallucinations.
