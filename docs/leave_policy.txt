TechNova Solutions – Leave Policy

Paid Time Off (PTO):
All full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per year, accruing at 1.67 days per month. PTO can be used for vacation, personal days, or any reason. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 10 days. PTO beyond 30 total days is forfeited at year-end.

Sick Leave:
Unlimited sick leave with manager approval. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate may be requested. Sick leave is not to be used for vacation.

Public Holidays:
TechNova observes 10 paid public holidays per year based on the employee's office location. Holiday calendars are published in January each year.

Parental Leave:
- Maternity Leave: 16 weeks fully paid, available to birth parents. Additional 4 weeks at 60% pay if needed.
- Paternity Leave: 8 weeks fully paid, available to non-birth parents.
- Adoption Leave: 12 weeks fully paid for adoption or foster care placement.
- Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.

Bereavement Leave:
5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, sibling). 3 paid days for extended family members.

Jury Duty:
Employees receive full pay during jury duty service. Provide your jury summons to HR.

Sabbatical:
Employees with 5+ years of tenure are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical. The sabbatical must be approved by the employee's VP and planned at least 3 months in advance.

Leave Request Process:
1. Submit leave request via NovaAssist portal or email to your manager.
2. Manager approves or discusses alternate dates.
3. HR records the approved leave.
4. For leaves longer than 2 weeks, a transition plan must be documented.
