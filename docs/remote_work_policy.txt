TechNova Solutions – Remote Work Policy

TechNova operates a hybrid work model that balances flexibility with collaboration.

Eligibility:
All full-time employees are eligible for remote work unless their role requires physical presence (e.g., office management, lab work). Eligibility is confirmed during onboarding.

Hybrid Schedule:
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- At least 2 days per week should be spent in the office (Tuesday and Thursday are recommended anchor days).
- Fully remote arrangements are available for employees located 50+ miles from the nearest office, subject to VP approval.

Equipment:
- All remote employees receive a company laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad, employee's choice).
- $2,500 one-time home office setup stipend for desk, chair, monitor, and accessories.
- $100/month internet stipend for remote work days.

Expectations:
- Maintain regular working hours (core hours: 10 AM – 4 PM in your local timezone).
- Be available on Slack and respond within 1 hour during core hours.
- Attend all scheduled team meetings via video with camera on.
- Maintain a professional and secure work environment.
- Use company VPN when accessing internal systems.

Security Requirements:
- Use company-provided laptop only (no personal devices for work).
- Enable disk encryption and screen lock.
- Do not work from public Wi-Fi without VPN.
- Follow clean desk policy for physical documents.

Fully Remote Employees:
Fully remote employees must visit their assigned office at least once per quarter for team events, planning sessions, and in-person collaboration.

International Remote Work:
Working from a country different from your employment country requires prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax and employment law implications. Maximum 30 days per year in a different country.
