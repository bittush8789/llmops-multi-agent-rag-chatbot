TechNova Solutions – Asset Policy

TechNova provides technology equipment to all employees. This policy governs the use, maintenance, and return of company assets.

Equipment Provided:
- Laptop: MacBook Pro 16" or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon (employee's choice). Refreshed every 3 years.
- Monitor: One 27" 4K monitor for office use. Remote employees can purchase with their home office stipend.
- Peripherals: Keyboard, mouse, headset provided upon request.
- Mobile: Company phone or $50/month BYOD stipend for roles requiring mobile access.

Software Licenses:
All software must be approved and provisioned by IT. Standard software includes: Microsoft 365, Slack, Zoom, GitHub, Figma, and all TechNova products. Additional software requests are submitted via NovaAssist.

Acceptable Use:
- Company equipment is for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable.
- Do not install unauthorized software or modify system configurations.
- Do not share your device with family members or non-employees.
- Keep your operating system and software updated.

Security:
- Full disk encryption must be enabled at all times.
- Screen lock must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Report lost or stolen devices to IT within 1 hour at security@technovasolutions.com.
- IT can remotely wipe lost devices.

Return Policy:
All equipment must be returned within 5 business days of employment termination. Shipping labels are provided for remote employees. Failure to return equipment may result in payroll deduction for the replacement cost.

Equipment Replacement:
For damaged or malfunctioning equipment, submit a ticket via NovaAssist. Replacement devices are shipped within 2 business days.

Contact: it@technovasolutions.com
