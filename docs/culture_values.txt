TechNova Solutions – Culture and Values

Our Five Core Values:

1. Build with Purpose
Everything we create must solve a real problem. We don't build technology for technology's sake. Every feature, every product, every line of code must serve a clear customer need.

2. Own the Outcome
We take full ownership of our work and its impact. No finger-pointing, no excuses. If something breaks, we fix it. If a customer is unhappy, we make it right.

3. Move Fast, Stay Grounded
Speed matters in technology. We ship quickly, iterate rapidly, and learn from failures. But we never compromise on quality, security, or customer trust.

4. Transparency by Default
We share information openly. Company financials, product roadmaps, and strategic decisions are communicated to all employees. We believe informed teams make better decisions.

5. Lift Each Other Up
We succeed as a team. We mentor, we teach, we celebrate each other's wins. Diversity of thought, background, and experience makes us stronger.

Culture Programs:
- Innovation Fridays: Employees spend every other Friday working on passion projects or learning new skills.
- Quarterly Hackathons: Cross-functional teams compete to build creative solutions. Winning ideas often become real product features.
- Nova Kudos: A peer recognition program where employees can nominate colleagues for living our values. Winners receive gift cards and public recognition.
- Monthly All-Hands: CEO Arjun Mehta hosts a company-wide meeting covering financials, product updates, customer wins, and open Q&A.
- Learning Budget: Every employee receives $2,000 per year for courses, conferences, books, or certifications.
- Wellness Program: Free access to mental health counseling, gym memberships, and wellness apps.

Employee Satisfaction:
Our 2025 employee engagement survey showed a 92% satisfaction rate. Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS) is 72, which is considered world-class.
