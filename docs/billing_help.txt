TechNova Solutions - Billing & Account Management FAQ

Billing Cycles:
- Monthly: Billed on the 1st of every month.
- Annual: Billed once per year with a 15% discount.

Accepted Payment Methods:
- Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX).
- Bank Wire (For Enterprise plans).
- ACH / Direct Debit.

Invoicing:
- All invoices are available in the 'Billing' section of the Admin Dashboard.
- Invoices are emailed automatically to the designated billing contact.

Account Cancellation:
- To cancel, navigate to 'Settings' -> 'Subscription' -> 'Cancel Plan'.
- Cancellation requests require 30 days notice.
- No prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations after the 30-day money-back period.

Updating Account Details:
- Admins can update company address, tax ID, and contact info in 'Settings' -> 'Account'.
- For ownership transfers, contact accounts@technovasolutions.com.
